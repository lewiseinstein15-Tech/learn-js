About Projectile Motion

Projectile motion is the motion of an object thrown or projected into the air, subject to only the acceleration of gravity. The object follows a curved path called a parabola. This simulator demonstrates how the launch angle, initial velocity, and gravitational acceleration affect the trajectory.

Key Formulas:
• Max Height: h = (v₀² × sin²θ) / (2g)
• Range: R = (v₀² × sin(2θ)) / g
• Flight Time: t = (2 × v₀ × sinθ) / g
