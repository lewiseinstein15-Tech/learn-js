How projectile motion works

We break the initial velocity into horizontal (v₀ cos θ) and vertical (v₀ sin θ) components. The horizontal velocity stays constant (ignoring air resistance), while gravity pulls the vertical velocity down at 9.8 m/s².

The projectile reaches max height when vertical velocity hits zero, and lands when vertical position returns to zero. We verified our results against the textbook formulas.

x(t) = v₀ · cos(θ) · t
y(t) = v₀ · sin(θ) · t − ½gt²
T = 2v₀sin(θ) / g
R = v₀²sin(2θ) / g
