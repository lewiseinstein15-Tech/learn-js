05 — about

About this project

This repo started as our way to learn JavaScript by building instead of just reading tutorials. It's a group project — everyone adds a page, a tool or a game, and we review each other's pull requests. Nothing here is perfect, and that's kind of the point: it's a living record of what we're learning.
