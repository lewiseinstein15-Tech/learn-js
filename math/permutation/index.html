Permutation Counter

Type a word and see how many distinct permutations exist. The math: divide the total factorial by the factorial of each repeated letter's count.

—

Breakdown

How it works

For a word with n letters where letter a repeats r₁ times, b repeats r₂ times, etc:

n! / (r₁! × r₂! × ... × rₖ!)

For example, "BANANA" has 6 letters with A appearing 3 times and N appearing 2 times.

6! / (3! × 2!) = 720 / (6 × 2) = 60

We built this because computing it by hand for our homework was taking forever. Now we can check our work in seconds.
