Why projects?

Where theory meets practice

The math tools and physics simulator taught us individual concepts. These projects forced us to put everything together — DOM manipulation, event handling, API calls, state management, and making things that actually work for daily use.

The calculator was our first real project. The todo list was our first time building something with a backend. Both have been updated and polished as we learned more.
